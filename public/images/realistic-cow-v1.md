# Realistic cow view

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool using the user's small cow-and-stomach reference image for visual direction. These are photorealistic educational illustrations, not real photographs of exposed anatomy or a replacement 3D mesh.

Assets saved in this project:

- `realistic-cow-cutaway-v1.png`: realistic cow with a digestive cutaway and the capsule inside the honeycomb reticulum.
- `realistic-cow-solid-v1.png`: matching intact-cow image for the transparent-stomach toggle.

The app retains its existing 3D anatomy under **Rotate 3D**. The selected cow, sensor readings, navigation, health analysis and farmer dashboard keep their existing behavior. The illustrated cow is representative of the simulated herd; it is not an identification photograph of a particular cow.

## Cutaway generation prompt

Use case: scientific-educational, photorealistic. Create a high-resolution wide landscape photographic cattle-health visualization for a simulation website. The attached tiny dashboard crop is ONLY a visual reference for the black-and-white dairy cow with a realistic abdominal cutaway; do not recreate its text, UI or branding. Primary subject: an anatomically realistic adult black-and-white Holstein dairy cow standing, full body clearly visible including all four hooves, udder, tail, ears and head. Cow in left-side profile facing right, with head slightly turned toward viewer. Healthy natural proportions, detailed short fur and irregular black patches, realistic nose, expressive eyes, subtle yellow ear tag. Cow fills most of the wide image, with generous margins around feet, head and tail. Setting: actual rustic South African cattle kraal with weathered wood fence, packed earth under the hooves, pasture and soft low hills behind, natural soft daylight, lifelike shadows. Avoid stylized CGI, cartoon, low-poly, toy-like shapes. On the visible left abdominal flank, use a clean oval medical cutaway window integrated into the animal: surrounding cow fur remains entirely opaque and photorealistic, the window reveals realistic moist warm reddish-pink digestive tissue. Include a large lobulated rumen occupying the left/rear of the exposed window and a smaller reticulum chamber at the right/front, toward the chest and lower abdomen. The reticulum shows its characteristic honeycomb mucosal texture. The smaller reticulum is itself partially cut away so a single small smooth silver and turquoise-green cylindrical rounded smart bolus capsule is visibly housed INSIDE it. Capsule is small relative to the organ, horizontally oriented, clearly discernible with a subtle green end indicator, not floating outside. The tissue should look like a polished lifelike veterinary educational illustration rather than simple coloured spheres, plastic organs or excessive gore. Show organ walls, depth, folds and softly lit interior detail. No diagram labels, no typography, no arrows, no logo, no UI, no watermark, no human, no extra animal. Aim for the photographic realism of the reference cow, with professionally composited medical cutaway. Wide 16:9 composition suitable for a 1000-by-500 webpage panel. Keep the cutaway centered approximately at 45 percent image width and 55 percent image height so it can be annotated by the app.
